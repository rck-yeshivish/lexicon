# Soul: nefesh / ruach / neshamah / chayah / yechidah

## Bottom line

One soul, five names, ascending (Bereishis Rabbah 14:9). *Nefesh*, the life
bound to blood and body, seat of appetite and will: **soul** (in Tanach also
**life**, **person**, **self**). *Ruach*, the breath-like middle tier, seat of
emotion and speech: **spirit**. *Neshamah*, Hashem's breath, the soul only man
has: **higher soul**. *Chayah* is the life-force that outlives the body, and
*yechidah* the singular point.

## Distinctions

### nefesh (נפש)
Root נ-פ-ש; over 750 occurrences. Rambam (Moreh 1:41): many senses, all
circling the soul: blood (Bamidbar 19:13, Devarim 12:23); a corpse, one who
has lost his *nefesh* (Vayikra 19:28, 21:1); death as the *nefesh* departing
(Bereishis 35:18; Chazal speak instead of the *neshamah* leaving); will and
wants (Rashi to Bereishis 23:8, Shemos 15:9, Mishlei 13:2); the self (Rashi
to Yechezkel 32:10). Pappenheim: from פ-ש, expansion, the soul that never
atrophies. The verb *vayinafash* (Shemos 31:17) is the *nefesh* catching its
breath (Rashi). Function: the bodily lifeline (Bereishis Rabbah 14:9); the
natural (Maharsha) or survival instinct (Vilna Gaon); man's deeds (Rabbi Chaim
of Volozhin, Nefesh HaChaim 1:14); the liver and bodily appetite (Ibn Ezra,
Rabbeinu Yonah, the Gaon); food, of a husband's three duties (Rabbi Yosef
Karo). Radak (Sefer HaShorashim): the
seat of desire, so the Torah charges sin to it (Vayikra 5:1, 22:6); *nefesh
rechavah* (Avos 5:19) is lust. In Nefesh HaChaim's glassblower (1:15) the
*nefesh* is the molten glass, last stop of the Divine breath, hence its verb
means rest.

### ruach (רוח)
Root ר-ו-ח; some 390 occurrences. Rambam (Moreh 1:40): first air (Bereishis
1:2), then wind, direction, the life-spirit (Bereishis 7:15) that survives
death (Koheles 12:7), prophecy (Bamidbar 11:17; Shmuel II 23:2), and wants or
temperament (Mishlei 29:11; anger in Iyov 15:13). Raavad (to Sefer Yetzirah):
not the air one breathes but a spiritual engine Hashem powers directly, hence
Moshe's אלקי הרוחות לכל בשר (Bamidbar 16:22, 27:16). Pappenheim: ר-ח is air,
the soul as light and imperceptible. Function: the part that flies up in
sleep (Bereishis Rabbah 14:9); the spiritual (Maharsha) or emotional (Gaon)
tier, the drive for gain; man's speech (Nefesh HaChaim 1:14); the heart and
its hunger for honor and wealth (Ibn Ezra, Rabbeinu Yonah, Gaon); clothing
(Rabbi Yosef Karo). *Ruach gevohah* (Avos 5:19) is haughtiness.

### neshamah (נשמה)
Root נ-ש-ם; only 25 occurrences, because it means the soul and nothing else,
and never of an animal (Ibn Ezra to Bereishis 7:21, Koheles 3:21; Radak to
Yeshayahu 42:5). Tied to *neshimah*, breathing (Bereishis Rabbah 14:9 on
Tehillim 150:6; Rashi to Yoma 85a), and by the sages Ibn Ezra cites to
*shamayim*; Pappenheim grounds both in ש-ם, the abstract (*sham*, *shem*);
Mecklenburg calls it the *geist*. Function: the sum of a man's personhood
(Bereishis Rabbah 14:9); the intellectual tier (Maharsha, Gaon); man's thought
(Nefesh HaChaim 1:14); the brain and its yearning for Hashem; intimacy, the
most private sustenance (Rabbi Yosef Karo). Rabbeinu Yonah: animals share
*nefesh* and *ruach*; the *neshamah* sets man apart. Radak (to Bereishis 2:7):
*nefesh* and *ruach* belong properly to the lower creatures and are said of
man only by extension. Rabbi Chaim of Volozhin (1:15): not respiration but
Hashem "blowing" life into man, the tier closest to Him.

### chayah (חיה) and yechidah (יחידה)
*Chayah* enters at Bereishis 2:7 (*nishmas chaim*, *nefesh chayah*; Onkelos:
*ruach memalela*, a speaking spirit); Nefesh HaChaim 2:17 calls it "the
*neshamah* of the *neshamah*." Pappenheim: ח-י, life, a state of harmony
among the body's parts. Bereishis Rabbah 14:9: the soul that goes on living
when the body dies; the Gaon (Aderes Eliyahu to Bereishis 2:7): the holistic
life-force. *Yechidah* never means soul in Tanach (Shoftim 11:34 is an only
daughter); Pappenheim's ח-ד is singularity (*echad*, *yachad*), and the
Midrash calls it the one limb with no counterpart. Who has them is disputed:
the Gaon, no *yechidah* until Mashiach; Shelah, only *bnei aliyah*; Rabbi
Alexander Sender Shor, only a prophet, while a sinner loses *neshamah*, then
*ruach*, and keeps an animal's *nefesh*; Rabbi Yaakov Yehoshua Falk, *chayah*
through mitzvos after maturity, *yechidah* in life to Moshe alone.

## Rendering guidance

- In Tanach let the sense govern *nefesh*: "life" (Bereishis 19:17), "person"
  or "soul" in counts, "self" as a reflexive, "appetite" where Rashi reads
  will. In a mussar text the word is "soul."
- The triad נפש רוח ונשמה: "soul, spirit, and higher soul," or transliterate
  all three with a one-time gloss; never let two of them share a word.
- *Nishmas chaim* (Bereishis 2:7): "breath of life"; *nefesh chayah* in the
  same verse: "living soul." *Chayah* and *yechidah* elsewhere: transliterate,
  glossed once as "the life-force" and "the singular soul."
- Trap: "soul" absorbs *nefesh* and *neshamah*; "spirit" (Mecklenburg's
  *geist*) and "breath" can each absorb *ruach* and *neshamah*. Where the
  passage ranks the soul's parts, one English word per Hebrew word.
