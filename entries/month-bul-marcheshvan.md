# The eighth month: Bul / Marcheshvan (mabul, geshem)

## Bottom line

**Bul** and **Marcheshvan** name the same month and both stay transliterated; in most of
Tanach it is simply *chodesh hashemini*, **the eighth month**, counted from Nissan. Bul is
the older, pre-exilic name (Melachim I 6:38); Marcheshvan never appears in Tanach and first
shows up in Mishnah. Do not translate either by an etymology, and in particular do not
render the *mar* of Marcheshvan as "bitter": whether *mar* is even a separate element is
disputed. In the same family, *mabul* is **flood** and *geshem* **rain**, and the
difference is not quantity.

## Where the authorities disagree

**What Bul means.** The Yerushalmi (Rosh Hashanah 1:2, and Targum Yonasan to Melachim I
6:38) gives three at once: *novel*, the leaves fall; *bul*, the earth turns to wet clods
with the first rains; *bolel*, one must mix fodder indoors because nothing is left in
the field. The Rid and Ibn Saruk: from *yevul*, produce, the month when the crop is
gathered in. Pappenheim: from *bilui*, wearing out. The *Pri Megadim*, on Radak's gloss
of *bul eitz* (Yeshayahu 44:19) as a branch: the eighth month branches off from Tishrei.
Rabbi Aharon Marcus reads *bul eitz* as an unusually thick tree and makes *bul*
extraordinary increase, hence *mabul* for excess water and Bul for the surplus after
harvest. Radak: from *mabul* itself, the month opening the rainy season, as in *Midrash
Tanchuma* (*Noach* §11), that until the Mikdash stood forty days of flood-rain fell each
year, and with its building the mem dropped from *mabul*.

**Whether mar is a separate element of Marcheshvan.** Those who parse it: the *Aruch
HaShulchan*, that *mar* means switch, as in *temurah*, for Yeravam's switching the seventh
month for the eighth, with the bitterness that followed as a second sense, though he
concludes one may not expound the name where halachah depends on it (his case: writing *Mar
Cheshvan* as two words in a *get*); and Rabbi Chizkiyahu Medini, who records a custom not to
make weddings in Marcheshvan because *mar* means bitter. Against them, the *Pri Chadash*
takes *mar* as drops of rain (Yeshayahu 40:15), or the bitterness of a month with no yom
tov, or of the passing of the Imahos, none of which bears on weddings; Rabbi Betzalel Stern
holds *mar* is not Hebrew at all; and the *Nachalas Shivah* rejects the parsing outright.
Rabbi Chaim Dov Lieman (*Otzar Peninim* §28) defends the custom from Rashi to Bereishis
Rabbah 48:9, that the month-names were revealed through nevuah and so may be expounded. For
translation the Bottom line follows the *Nachalas Shivah* and Rabbi Betzalel Stern.

**What Cheshvan means, for those who parse it.** Rav Hirsch: ח-ש-ה, quiet, the hush after
Tishrei. The *Sefer HaKushyos*: ר-ח-ש, stirring, or ש-ו-ה, evened out in its rains. Rabbi
Zecharia HaRofeh (*Midrash HaChefetz*): the Aramaic *israchish*, an occurrence, the first
Mikdash having been completed in it. Rabbi Yisrael of Ruzhin: our lips are still *rocheshes*
from the tefillos of the month before.

**Where the name came from.** The Yerushalmi and Ramban (to Shemos 12:2) hold the names in
use today came up from Bavel. Rabbi Ernest Klein and Rabbi Dr. Ari Zivotofsky read
Marcheshvan as one word meaning "eighth month," Zivotofsky noting that Yemenite Jews say
Marach-Shwan; Rabbi Shaul Goldman suggests instead a link to *shemen* and the olive
pressing. The Maharal's system joins the two: seven is nature and eight what stands above
it, and oil rises above water as the supernatural rises above the natural.

## Distinctions

### Bul (בול)
Melachim I 6:38, the completion of the Beis HaMikdash "in the month of Bul, which is the
eighth month," the one occurrence in Tanach; elsewhere the month is only *chodesh
hashemini* (Melachim I 12:32-33, Zechariah 1:1).

### Marcheshvan (מרחשון)
Absent from Tanach; the earliest source is Mishnah Taanis 1:3-4. The *mabul* began in this
month (Rosh Hashanah 11b; Rashi to Bereishis 7:11-12), and in it Yeravam set up a
counterfeit Sukkos to keep his people from Yerushalayim (Melachim I 12:32-33).

### mabul (מבול) and geshem (גשם)
Rashi (to Bereishis 6:17): a *mabul* wastes everything (*bilui*), confounds everything
(*bilbul*) and carries things from above to below (*movil*), so he too builds it on ב-ל.
Rabbi Moshe Shapiro: *geshem* is connection, heaven to earth plainly and *tzurah* to
*chomer* deeper, and the same water descends either as *mabul* or as *geshem* according
as the generation deserves rain as blessing (Rashi to Bereishis 7:12).

## Rendering guidance

- Render the month names by transliteration: **Bul**, **Marcheshvan**, **Cheshvan**. Where
  Tanach says *chodesh hashemini*, translate it, **the eighth month**.
- The trap is "the eighth month" absorbing the name Bul. Melachim I 6:38 has both in one
  verse, *b'yerach Bul hu hachodesh hashemini*, and writing "the eighth month" twice erases
  the pasuk's own gloss.
- Never render Marcheshvan as "bitter Cheshvan": that is one disputed reading of one
  disputed element, and the poskim who reject it are the majority here.
- *Mabul* is **the Flood** in Bereishis and **flood** elsewhere; *geshem* is **rain**. Do
  not let one English word cover both: they are the same water under two verdicts.
